# Wiki authoring

- Always include a References section when creating a wiki page.
- Cite sources near the definitions, theorems, algorithms, complexity claims, and historical claims they support. Prefer primary sources, textbooks, and official documentation, and use working links when available.
- Verify source content before citing it. Distinguish worked examples and derivations from quotations; do not invent references or page numbers.
- Follow the existing wiki front matter and use `relative_url` for links between wiki entries.
